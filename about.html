Project: Event Horizon

The Mission

It is inspired by rail shooters from the other games, like SF64, to SF Zero. This game includes voice acting, astonishing music, and memorable characters.

Key Features

  • Restored History: Defeat Andross during the second Lylat War, save Sauria from the sharpclaws, and figure out who's behind after the Thosan incident.
  • Pilots: Meet the veterans of the Lylat System: the leader of the team, Fox. To his former rival, Wolf. And many, many more.
  • Massive Scale: Engage with colossal bosses and capital-class dreadnoughts. From the Attack Carrier, to a robot in space, or going underwater.

Development

This mod is mainly developed by UndyingNephalim and started way back in 2014 as an RTS (Real-Time Strategy). Over time, he slowly changed the formula. Using Freespace Open as its engine, the game went from multiple playable characters to now having 3 playable campaigns.
