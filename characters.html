Team Star Fox

Fox McCloud

Leader

The leader of the Star Fox team. Fox balances a sense of duty with elite piloting skills, leading the fight against the Venomian threat.

Falco Lombardi

Ace Pilot

Ace pilot of the Star Fox team. Can be a bit of a short-tempered, but always counting on helping his friends.

Peppy Hare

Veteran Pilot

The original member of the Star Fox team with James and Pigma. After being betrayed by Pigma and barely escaping, he returned home and told James's son, Fox, about his father's faith to the team.

Slippy Toad

Starfox Engineer

The engineer of Starfox. Slippy met Fox during the academy days and joined the team to help analyze enemies' weak points and upgrade weapons for the Arwings.
