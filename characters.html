Personnel Database

Team Star Fox

Fox McCloud

Leader

The leader of the Star Fox team. Fox balances a sense of duty with elite piloting skills, leading the fight against the Venomian threat.

Falco Lombardi

Ace Pilot

The ace pilot of the team. He can be a bit short-tempered, but he always helps out his friends.

Peppy Hare

Veteran Pilot

The original member of the Star Fox team with James and Pigma. After finding out Andross is back, he joined the team with James's son, Fox.

Slippy Toad

Engineer

The engineer of the team. Specializing in weapon upgrades and analyzing weakpoints, but can be a bit too scientific with his words.

Team Star Wolf

Wolf O'Donnell

Commander

Leader of the Star Wolf mercenaries. A ruthless rival to Fox McCloud who commands the powerful Wolfen interceptor.

Leon Powalski

Assassin

A cold, calculated pilot known for his precision. Leon is perhaps the only pilot in the Lylat system who can match Falco's ego.
