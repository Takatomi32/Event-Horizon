SECOND LYLAT WAR — LEVELS

Corneria Orbit

An orbital battle above Corneria. Enemy fighters pour in from all directions as the Cornerian fleet struggles to hold the line. Debris fields, capital ship fire, and atmospheric glow create a chaotic battlefield in low orbit.

Objective: Intercept the invading forces and prevent them from breaching Corneria’s atmosphere.

Corneria City

Fourth planet of the Lylat System, and one of the very first inhabited worlds. A fully developed and civilized world. Corneria is largely commercial. It's the center of the government and military for the army, as well as a prime target for enemies.

Objective: Provide air support to Cornerian forces and drive enemy units out of the city center.

Sector Y

Sector Y is a large gaseous ring at the very edge of the Lylat System. Orbiting around Solar and Lylat Prime, bringing Sector Y close to Corneria, Katina, Fichina, and Aquas. Corneria uses Sector Y as its staging area for its fleets, depending on its distance from enemy targets

Objective: Escort General Pepper and its fleet through Sector Y, while waiting for the remaining survivors.

Meteo

Rare class C asteroid field. The contents are dangerously close to each other, making navigation difficult. Many ships prefer to go over or under the belt, though they can sneak through the hazards to catch the enemy off guard.

Objective: Navigate the asteroid field and break through to the outer sectors while avoiding debris and enemy ambushes.

Fichina

Has been in the grounds of the Cornerian Army for many projects. Even though the atmosphere can be suited for breathing, the Cornerian had little luck with the sub-zero temperatures. This planet has turned into a prime location for hazardous experiments that resulted in the formation of the Cornerian Science Institute.

Objective: Head down to the outpost after receiving a distress signal.

Katina

Long served as Corneria's first colony and military training base. The orbit is close to Corneria, and can be seen from the surface every sixty-three years. It's also known for its fungal growths, spreading across the surface of the planet for hundreds of miles during autumn seasons.

Objective: Assist the Cornerian Army to hold off the enemy lines.

Aquas

Homeworld of the frog-like Aquan people. After a cataclysmic disaster nearly destroyed the planet, the Aquan culture shifted and devoted its technology and resources to repairing the damaged ecosystem. Centuries later, the oceans of Aquas have nearly recovered.

Objective: Head down to Aquas and assist them.

Macbeth

The first planet of the Lylat System, Macbeth. For many years, it served as a weapon production and storage world for the Cornerian Army until it fell into the hands of Venom. Ever since the outbreak of the First Lylat War, the planet has been a battleground for many factions who wish to raid the world's huge cache of weaponry for themselves.

Objective: Take down the forever train.

Sector X

Sector X is part of the large gaseous ring at the very edge of the Lylat System. The orbit of Solar around Lylat Prime occasionally brings the majority of Sector X near Fichina and Aquas. It has a tendency to cause electromagnetic disturbances and mess with scanning devices, making it an ideal location to hide fleets and even entire bases from enemies.

Objective: Intercept the base, and take out the secret weapon.

Zoness

Zoness was once a sister colony of the Aquan people. Much like their homeworld, they crippled the planet's ecosystem with devastating toxic waste and eventually abandoned the planet. Centuries later, both the Cornerian Army and later Venom used the former Aquan planet as a waste disposal world.

Objective: Head down to Aquas and assist them.
