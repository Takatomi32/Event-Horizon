Level 1 — Corneria Orbit

A fast‑paced orbital battle above Corneria. Enemy fighters pour in from all directions as the Cornerian fleet struggles to hold the line. Debris fields, capital ship fire, and atmospheric glow create a chaotic battlefield in low orbit.

Objective: Intercept the invading forces and prevent them from breaching Corneria’s atmosphere.
