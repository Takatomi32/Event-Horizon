SECOND LYLAT WAR — LEVELS

Level 1 — Corneria Orbit

An orbital battle above Corneria. Enemy fighters pour in from all directions as the Cornerian fleet struggles to hold the line. Debris fields, capital ship fire, and atmospheric glow create a chaotic battlefield in low orbit.

Objective: Intercept the invading forces and prevent them from breaching Corneria’s atmosphere.

Level 2 - Corneria City

The battle descends into Corneria’s capital as enemy forces overrun the streets. Cornerian troops struggle to hold defensive lines while civilians attempt to flee the chaos.

Objective: Provide air support to Cornerian forces and drive enemy units out of the city center.

Level 3A - Sector Y

A classified weapons testing zone filled with experimental enemy units. Prototype walkers, drone swarms, and biomechanical fighters flood the battlefield as the team pushes through the heart of the research sector.

Objective: Destroy the experimental forces and disrupt the enemy’s weapons development program.

Level 3B - Meteo

A dense and unstable asteroid field filled with drifting debris, collapsing tunnels, and sudden hazards. Enemy fighters weave through the rocks as the team pushes deeper into the chaotic region. Precision flying and quick reactions are essential to survive the shifting terrain.

Objective: Navigate the asteroid field and break through to the outer sectors while avoiding debris and enemy ambushes.
