SAURIA CRISIS — LEVELS

Yume Moon

Sauria's only remaining satellite, Yume, shows the scars of its collision with the planet's other smaller moon countless millennia ago. Yume also bears a ring; more remnants from its ancient sister that still occasionally fall to the surface of Sauria. The moon itself contains strange ores that disrupt most known scanning equipment.

Objective: Head down to Sauria, find the source of the signal, while avoiding the enemy fleet.

Warlock Mountain

A large natural edifice looming on the peninsula coast of Sauria's western continent, Warlock Mountain is crowned by the mysterious Krazoa Palace. The region around the mountain is completely untamed, while just across the channel to the east lies Sauria's many Dinosaur Tribes.

Objective: Head to the main source of the signal.

Golden Plains

The dry Golden Plains sprawl along the western coast of Sauria's inhabited continent, shining brightly like precious metal at high noon. Though they give off the appearance of being barren, amongst the many Krazoa Ruins can be found nomadic bands of the cranky Bonehead Tribe.

Objective: Pass through the desert en route to the Force Point Temple.

Cape Claw

One of the most pleasant beach fronts on the entire planet, once served as the far western edge of the Cloudrunner Kingdoms. An ancient Cloudrunner Fortress still stands in ruins to this very day, long abandoned by its creators and now in the hands of the Sharpclaw.

Objective: Pass through the skies of Cape Claw, en route to the Force Point Temple

Force Point Temple

Mysterious remains of the long-vanished Krazoa, the five Force Point Temples once served as centers of the ancient Saurian religion. The knowledge of their function has long since been lost, and only two of these Temples remain. The temple just east of the Golden Plains and Cape Claw is haunted by mystical ghosts and is rumored to house one of Sauria's lost Spellstones.

Objective: Race against the sharpclaw to retrieve the Spellstone.

Willow Grove

The dark and foreboding Willow Grove is an area avoided by all who can help it. Willow Grove became a nightmare after Dragon Rock crashed nearby in ancient times. Dark magics from the meteor continue to warp and disfigure the flora and fauna that enter the Grove, transforming them into horrific monsters.

Objective: Search for a lost Star Fox member.

Thorntail Hollow

Home of the peaceful Thorntails, the Hollow itself serves as the northern border of the Earthwalker Empire. The Thorntails are usually left to their own devices, and the region gives off a sense of calm to travelers who wander through. The enigmatic Warpstone, a living construct built by the Krazoa, has slept here for countless centuries.

Objective: Find the Warpstone.

Discovery Falls

There is an ancient Saurian proverb that says, 'One of pure heart will always find what they seek at Discovery Falls.' This huge cliffscape displays impressive cascading waterfalls entwined with massive Magic Gem Plants. Few Krazoa ruins dot the river flowing from the falls, but the surrounding valley remains otherwise pristine against Sauria's wild backdrop.

Objective: Seek out Cloudrunner survivors along the rivers of Sauria.

Lightfoot Forest

The dark and swampy homelands of the Lightfoot Tribe, these hostile and reclusive Dinosaurs do all that they can to keep outsiders away from their territory. While the Cloudrunners effortlessly fly over the forest to reach their holdings, the river shortcut through the canopy-covered marshes is avoided by those stuck on land for fear of becoming the Lightfoot Tribe's next meal.

Objective: Find Prince Tricky.

Twilight Marshes

Stretching from horizon to horizon, the massive wetlands of the Twilight Marshes surround the southern regions of the Earthwalker Empire and mark the edge of the known world. Deep in the uncharted south dwells the mysterious Razortooth Tribe, of which no Dinosaur has seen in centuries.

Objective: Cross the border wetlands to reach the heart of the Earthwalker tribe.

Diamond Bay

A sweeping, majestic coast at the southeastern edge of the main Saurian continent, Diamond Bay houses many crumbling Krazoa Ruins and is home to the grumpy Pointback Tribe. Amongst the rubble are massive cannon-like structures, hinting at a siege taking place here long before even the Saurian legends.

Objective: Fly through dangerous ruins and beaches to reach the Earthwalker tribe.

Snowhorn Wastes

Named after the hearty Tribe that dwells here, the Wastes drape along all sides of the twin Snowhorn Mountains. A region so cold that it snows all year long, nothing but the woolly Snowhorns and sparse flora live in this desolate place. Long serving as the dividing line between Cloudrunner and Earthwalker lands, the Sharpclaw have recently taken an interest in digging deep under these icy mountains...

Objective: Help the Snowhorn Tribe.

Moon Mountain Pass

The Moon Mountain range is a hostile, strange, and alien landscape. Formed from the smaller moon of Sauria colliding with Yume and then falling to the planet, this rough region has long served as the homeland of the vicious Sharpclaw Tribe. The Sharpclaw themselves claim they originated on the destroyed moon, surviving the planetfall.

Objective: Escape the Sharpclaw homelands.

Blackwater Canyon

At the heart of the Blackwater Canyon lies the Tree of Souls, perhaps the most sacred place in all of Sauria. Many Dinosaurs believe the spirits of the dead flow down the rivers through these canyons until coming to rest at the Tree of Souls. The Shadow Hunter Tribe has dedicated their entire culture to ferrying the departed and protecting the tree from the living world.

Objective: Seek safe passage to the Cloudrunner kingdoms.

Walled City

The heart of the Earthwalker Empire, the imposing Walled City currently serves as their capital and homeland. In ancient times, the Walled City was built for the purpose of a sacrificial altar to the tyrannical Redeye Tribe, though after generations of servitude, their Earthwalker slaves led a massive revolt and overthrew the giant Dinosaurs.

Objective: Help the Earthwalker Tribe retake Walled City from Scales and the Redeye tribe.

Cloudrunner Fortress

Perched on top of the highest mountain in the northernmost regions of Sauria, the Cloudrunner Fortress is the seat of power of the Cloudrunner Kingdoms and the homeland of their Tribe. Its location has made the Fortress and its city virtually impervious to any land attack. The mountaintop is considered one of the most beautiful places in all of Sauria, an opinion the Cloudrunners flaunt whenever they get the chance.

Objective: Bring the Fortress back to peace.

Volcano Force Point Temple

Homeland of the giant Redeye Tribe, this massive volcano is all that is left of their ancient empire. Though the surrounding land is quite fertile, the powerful magics that come from the Force Point Temple within the volcano keep the mountain itself inhospitable. The Temple that rests here is unique among the others; it has an altar for all four of the Krazoa Spellstones.

Objective: Lure Scales and the Sharpclaw army away from the temple.

Dragon Rock

A meteor that crashed into Sauria before the time of legends, dark magic from Dragon Rock ravages the lands around its ominous crater. The meteor is an egg of a Great Dragon, a weapon used by the Kamerians in their long-forgotten war against the Krazoa to destroy entire planets. For mysterious reasons, this Great Dragon was never hatched, though Drakor plans to encourage its long-prophesied birth.

Objective: Defeat General Scales before the dragon egg hatches.

Kamerian Heart

Deep within Dragon Rock rests the artificial womb of the Great Dragon, a nightmarish technology of the Kamerians that can grow cosmic horrors the galaxy has not witnessed in eons. Using their own Spellstones against them, Drakor attempts to revive the dormant superweapon to continue his war against the Krazoa. If successful, all of Sauria will be destroyed in the process.

Objective: Stop Drakor and the Great Dragon.
